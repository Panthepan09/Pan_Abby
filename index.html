King

The King is your ally. Every time you click him, he gives you 10 points to help you win. He wants you to reach your goal before time runs out.

Queen

'The Queen is tricky. She does not want you to win. If you click her, you will lose 5 points. Try your best to stay away from her.

Son

The Son is strange. He can reset everything in a second. Click him, and your game will restart. Your score will clear, and the timer starts over again..


Reaction game


1.CONTENT

This is a fun reaction speed test game. It tests how quickly you can click the picture once it shows up on the screen. Your reaction speed will be measured in milliseconds. Try your best and see how fast you are!

2.HOW TO PLAY

First, press the START button. The picture will appear at a random place on the page. Click the picture right away when you see it. After you click, the game will show your reaction time. You can play many times to try to get a better score.
